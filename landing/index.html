How it works

From a sentence to your timeline in three steps.

No new app to learn. It's a panel inside Premiere — you type, it builds, it lands.

STEP 01

Describe it

Type what you want in plain English — “a bold stat callout that says 87%, dark, 3 seconds.” Attach a reference image if you have one.

make a 3-step checklist, kinetic ⏎
STEP 02

Claude builds it

It writes a fresh Remotion composition, renders an MP4 — or a transparent MOV — and checks the frame itself before handing it back.

STEP 03

It lands on your timeline

One click drops it on V2 or V3 right at your playhead. Want it different? Hit Changes and Claude tweaks it in place.

What's in the panel

Three ways to create, one place to do it.

Generate graphics, edit footage, and clean up speech — all without leaving Premiere or touching a timeline tool you don't want to.

Prompt → motion graphics

Titles, lower thirds, callouts, kinetic type, logo stings — built fresh each time with Remotion, never a template with the text swapped.

Auto-Edit

Point it at a talking-head clip. It reads the speech and places relevant graphics across the whole video — timed to exactly what's being said.

Auto-Cut

Strips silences, filler words and false starts in one pass, ripple-deleting cleanly across every track. One button, full undo.

Up to 10 versions at once

Not sure of the direction? Generate several distinct takes from one prompt and pick the winner — they render one after another, safely.

Ask Questions mode

Flip it on and Claude interviews you first — ratio, mood, motion, the bits it's unsure about — so the result matches what's in your head.

Iterate in place

Hit Changes on any render and tweak it — “make the title red, slower” — and Claude edits that exact piece instead of starting over.

Your Claude plan, no API key

It runs on your existing Claude Pro or Max subscription. Nothing to wire up, no key to paste, no per-render usage fees.

Installs in one click

A guided installer sets up everything for you. macOS and Windows, Premiere Pro 2024 and newer. No terminal, no fiddling.
